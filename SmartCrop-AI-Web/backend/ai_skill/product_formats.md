# Product-format playbook

The app adds only the section(s) that match the user's message. Each gives a typical starting shape for that kind of product, using database categories (Millet, Cereal, Legume, Pulse, Oilseed, Sweetener, Hydrocolloid, Seasoning, Dairy) so it stays true when crops are added. Bands are typical starting points, not rules: the goal, the caps and the clean-label status decide the final blend.

## Biscuit, cookie, cracker
<!-- keywords: biscuit, cookie, cracker, khakhra, mathri, rusk, nankhatai, baked snack -->
Base flours 55-75% combined, using two or more (a Millet plus a Cereal) because gluten-free flours alone give a crumblier bite. Protein booster from Pulse, Legume or Oilseed 8-22%. Sweetener 8-18% for sweet biscuits, none for crackers. A little Oilseed (3-8%) for richness. Salt up to 1.5%. No gum. Gap to state: no fat, leavening or water in the database.

## Flatbread, roti, paratha
<!-- keywords: roti, chapati, flatbread, paratha, thepla, phulka, bhakri -->
Dough needs cohesion. Wheat is the only gluten source in the database, so a gluten-free flatbread needs Millet or rice bases plus a binder; the only binder is a Hydrocolloid, so state that trade-off when clean-label is requested. Bases 70-90% combined, Pulse or Legume protein 8-20%, salt up to 1.5%, no sweetener.

## Pasta, noodle, vermicelli
<!-- keywords: pasta, noodle, vermicelli, macaroni, spaghetti, sev, seviyan -->
Needs a gluten network or a binder. With wheat: wheat base 50-70%, Pulse protein 15-30%. Gluten-free: rice base plus Pulse flours plus a small Hydrocolloid (1-2%), and say a gum is being used. Savoury, so no sweetener. Salt 1-2%. Texture after cooking is a lab question; say so.

## Extruded snack, puff, chips
<!-- keywords: extruded, extrusion, puff, puffed, crisps, chips, namkeen, popped -->
Starch-rich base (Cereal, Millet) 60-80%. Protein 10-25%: a lot of protein, fat or fibre reduces puffing, so keep fat-rich crops (Oilseed, whole Legume) small. Salt 1-2%. Sweetener optional and small. No gum needed.

## Porridge, instant mix, health mix
<!-- keywords: porridge, instant, sattu, malt, khichdi, daliya, breakfast, health mix, ready-to-cook -->
3-6 crops. Millet or Cereal base 45-70%, Pulse or Legume 15-30%, Oilseed 3-10%. Sweetener optional up to its cap; skip salt or keep it tiny. Whole soybean needs heat treatment, so mention it. For children or infants, say that lab and regulatory (FSSAI) validation is essential and make no suitability claim.

## Bar, ladoo, chikki
<!-- keywords: bar, ladoo, laddu, chikki, granola, energy bite, protein bar -->
Binding is the hard part: the only sweetener is jaggery and its cap limits stickiness, so say so. Oilseed as high as the caps allow (about 20-35% combined), roasted Pulse or Millet flour 30-50% for body, no gum. Present the result as a best effort within the database.

## Nugget, patty, tikki, meat analogue
<!-- keywords: nugget, patty, tikki, cutlet, kebab, burger, sausage, meat analogue, vegan meat, plant-based chicken, plant based chicken -->
Protein-dense: Legume, Pulse and Oilseed protein sources together 45-65%, with a Cereal or Millet 20-35% for bulk and binding. A small Hydrocolloid (up to its cap) as binder if the shape will not hold without it, and say so. Savoury: no sweetener, salt 1-2%. Defatted soy flour is a `check` item (hexane), so for clean-label use whole soybean (needs heat treatment), chickpea, moong, lentil or groundnut instead. Gaps to state: fibrous meat-like texture, fat, spices and coating are process or ingredients the database does not hold.

## Dairy alternative, khoya, paneer
<!-- keywords: khoya, mawa, paneer, curd, yogurt, cheese, milk substitute, dairy substitute, dairy alternative, vegan milk -->
Plant protein plus fat stand-ins: whole Legume, Oilseed and Pulse flours, with Sweetener for sweet khoya-style products and a small Hydrocolloid for body. Leave out the Dairy category because the product is plant-based, unless the user explicitly asks for milk powder. The database has no oil or nuts, so fat mouthfeel and texture are unproven; say so.

## General (fallback)
<!-- keywords: -->
Base 45-70% (Millet and Cereal), protein 10-30% (Pulse, Legume, Oilseed), everything else small: sweetener only if the product is sweet, Hydrocolloid only if something must bind, salt up to 2%. State the product format you assumed.
