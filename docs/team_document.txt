SmartCrop.AI "Turns local crops into optimized clean-label food formulations in seconds, acting as an AI R&D co-pilot for food innovators." 1. The Problem Statement Plant-based food products often require extensive trial-and-error during formulation. Startups may spend significant time testing binders, texture agents, protein sources, and ingredient combinations. Simultaneously, many regions have underutilized local crops that could provide highly useful nutritional and functional properties if leveraged correctly. 2. The Proposed Solution SmartCrop.AI is a lightweight, Streamlit-based AI food R&D assistant. A user enters a target food product, region, and specific formulation goals. The system combines a localized crop/ingredient database with structured prompting (via Google Gemini API) to generate a candidate clean-label formulation. R&D Co-Pilot, Not a Replacement: SmartCrop.AI is designed to accelerate early-stage decision making. All generated formulations act as highly optimized starting points requiring food-safety, sensory, nutritional, and laboratory validation before commercial use. 3. Expected Impact Help food innovators quickly generate and compare candidate formulations. Encourage and maximize the use of locally available agricultural crops. Drastically reduce early-stage trial-and-error time and associated costs. Support the rapid development of cleaner-label, sustainable plant-based foods. 🎙 Judges Pitch (Hindi/English Translation) "SmartCrop.AI is not just a recipe generator.
